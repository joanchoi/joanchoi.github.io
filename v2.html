액션

결과

Case studyA/B testing conversion paths cut cost per booking 40%
Meta AdsA/B TestUTMFunnel
Context
A Korean medicine clinic's ads produced a steady flow of event sign-ups (leads), but only about 7% turned into bookings. Performance was judged on lead count and cost per lead alone, so budget could be going to paths that never reached a booking.
Hypothesis
Compared with in-ad lead forms, a landing page where people read about the treatment and offer before signing up would cost more per lead but convert to bookings at a higher rate, making each booking cheaper.
Test
Ran two campaigns with the same creative and targeting, differing only in conversion path, side by side for a month, and tracked each path from lead to booking with UTMs. Booking rate was set as the primary metric and cost per booking as secondary before launch, and results were read two weeks after the test ended so bookings could settle. Lead forms brought 555 leads at ₩31,457 each and the landing page 126 at ₩77,334, but booking rates were 4% vs 20%, a statistically significant gap, putting cost per booking at about ₩790K vs ₩390K.
Action
The next month I shifted spend toward the landing page (126 → 293 leads, lead forms 555 → 210) and optimized it, bringing its cost per lead down 28% from ₩77,334 to ₩55,547.
Result
Leads fell from 681 to 503, yet bookings rose 41% (47 → 67), the booking rate went from about 7% to 13%, ad spend dropped 16% (about ₩27.2M → ₩22.9M) and cost per booking fell 40% (about ₩570K → ₩340K).
